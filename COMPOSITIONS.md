Because a filled disc's ink is its whole edge rather than a glyph floating inside a button, so the 48 square that centres on it leaves 12px of dead screen at the corner; `paddingLeft 4` / `paddingRight 16` with `MIN` alignment holds the disc where the optical rule wants it while the target still reaches the edge. - Sheet and drawer corners, `Visit/Drawer` 48:897, `Visit/Drawer edge` 70:1480, `Account/Microphone sheet` 121:1793. Every bottom sheet and drawer carries **32 top corners, 0 bottom**, `clipsContent` on. Because a surface rising from the bottom edge reads as a sheet only if its top corners are cut, and 16 read as slight. Nothing clamps: Figma caps radius at half the smaller side and the smallest carrier is 390 x 112. **Two things the radius forces, and neither is optional.** (1) *Over a light surface the sheet needs an edge*: a boolean `Stroke` property, default false, showing a **`color/divider` #D6D6D6 1px INSIDE top stroke** - the same construction as the Start recording footer keyline. It has to be a child node, not a stroke on the sheet itself, because a boolean property can only toggle visibility; the child is 390 x 64 absolute at (0,0), top corners 32, `strokeTopWeight 1` and the other sides 0, and it is 64 tall so the 32 radius is not clamped to 16. On for a drawer docked under a light bar, off over a field or a scrim. (2) *A docked drawer needs something behind its corners*: where a drawer meets a coloured bar, the bar's fill is extended **32 px down behind the drawer** as a frame-local rectangle inserted between bar and drawer in z-order, carrying the bar's own bound fill. Without it the corner cuts through to the page background and reads as two pale nicks. **Code carries neither value** - no `shape` key, no `MuiDrawer` or `MuiPaper` override, no radius in `DialogOverrides.ts` - so both are additions, not overrides. **One top for both drawers**: puller zone 30 over a title row 48, wherever the drawer appears - docked under a bar or opened over a field. Only the close button differs, on the `Closable` boolean, which after D1 drives nothing else. A drawer that docks under a light bar shows the stroke and hides the close; one that rises over a field shows the close and hides the stroke. Daniel, 2026-09-16.
- Account identity block, 140:1534. A person's name and their organisation laid out as a **two-line list row rather than a centred profile header**: 390 x 72, padding 16/8/16/8, a 56-wide lead column with the 40 avatar at x 16, text column at x 72 and 302 wide - the lead column and text origin a library `List/Item` `Lines=Two` uses when its icon is shown, measured off an instance rather than guessed. Name **Akkurat LL Regular 24 / 36**, matching the top bar title, which binds no published style either; organisation **Regular 14 / 20** bound `color/text/secondary`, taken from the row's own Secondary node so the two agree by construction. 36 + 20 = 56 and 8 + 8 = 16, so the block lands on 72 by arithmetic - the same height as the rows beneath it, which is what makes the identity read as the first row of the list rather than a banner above it. The avatar is 40 because there is no larger one (`mobileAccount_noLargeAvatarIdentityUsesForty`), so the 24pt name carries the whole hierarchy. Overrides nothing; it reproduces a library row's geometry around parts the row cannot hold. Daniel, 2026-09-16.
- Choose-from-a-list glyph, `Icon/DownCaret`. **One glyph for every control that opens a list**, because that is what code ships: `SelectProps.IconComponent` and Autocomplete's `popupIcon` are both `DownCaretIcon`, every list-opening `endIcon` is a `DownCaretIcon`, and `ArrowDropDown` appears nowhere in `packages/design-system`. Stock MUI's filled triangle is not this system's. Two sizes, each taking its colour from what it sits in: **20 bound `color/grey/600`** where it follows a heading, matching the Text field's own Select caret (the drawer's sheet selector); **16 on the button's label ink** inside a pill (Template, device). Gap to the label is **8** in both. **The cost, recorded rather than discovered later**: the caret is now spent, so it cannot also mean expand-in-place the way MUI's `ExpandMore` does - a future accordion or show-more needs its own affordance (`mobileVisit_caretCannotAlsoMeanExpand`). Daniel, 2026-09-16.
